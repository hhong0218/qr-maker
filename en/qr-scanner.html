What is this tool

QR Code Scanner reads the content already stored inside an existing QR code. It is especially useful for a QR code printed on a business card, stuck to a poster, or saved as an image file, in any situation where lining up your phone's camera app is awkward. Upload a photo or point your computer's webcam at the code, and the URL, text, Wi-Fi details, or whatever it contains appears right away. Need to make a new QR code instead? How to use it

To scan from an image, open the "Upload image" tab and choose a photo containing a QR code, or drag one onto the drop zone. Scanning starts automatically the moment you select a file, and the decoded content appears within a couple of seconds. To scan live, open the "Scan with camera" tab, click "Start camera," allow the browser's camera permission prompt, and center the QR code in the camera view; it is recognized automatically. If the result is a URL, the "Open link" button takes you there, and the "Copy to clipboard" button lets you paste the text anywhere else.

Is it safe

Yes. Any image you upload or camera frame you scan is never sent to a server; QR decoding runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. That said, the safety of what the QR code actually contains depends on whoever created it. When the scan result is a URL, this tool never navigates there automatically. It always shows a warning that the link leads to an external site before you click. If you scanned a QR code from an unknown source, double-check that the domain looks legitimate before opening any link.

Frequently asked questions

Q. How do I scan a QR code?

There are two ways to scan a QR code here. First, upload a photo that contains a QR code and it is decoded instantly. Second, tap "Start camera" to turn on your webcam and point it at a QR code for live, real-time recognition. This is handy whenever your phone's camera app is not convenient to use, when you want to check the content of a QR image you already have saved, or when you simply want to read a QR code quickly from a computer.

Q. Is the image I upload sent to a server?

No. Any image you upload or camera frame you scan is analyzed entirely inside your browser and is never sent to a server. QR decoding runs completely in client-side JavaScript, so even if you scan a QR code containing a business card or personal information, there is no risk of that content leaving your device.

Q. Is it safe to click a link found inside a scanned QR code?

Anyone can create a QR code, so a link decoded from one is not automatically safe. If the QR code is from an unknown or untrusted source, check the domain carefully before clicking. This tool never opens a decoded link automatically. Instead, it shows a warning that the link leads to an external site before you choose to open it.

Q. What if camera scanning does not work?

Camera scanning requires you to grant camera permission in your browser. Check the camera icon near your address bar to confirm access was allowed. This feature also requires a secure HTTPS connection, and it will not work on a device with no camera or when another program is already using the camera. If you cannot grant camera access, take a photo of the QR code instead and use the image upload option.

Q. Can this scan every type of QR code?

Yes, it can read most standard QR codes, including those containing a URL, plain text, Wi-Fi credentials, or a contact card (vCard). Recognition can fail if the image is too blurry, damaged, or if glare from lighting covers the code. In that case, try retaking the photo so the QR code appears large and sharp, or move the camera closer to the code while scanning.
